Privacy Policy for TrackSpend

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Last Updated: October 25, 2026
Application: TrackSpend: Expense & Income

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

At TrackSpend, your financial privacy is paramount. TrackSpend is designed from the ground up as a 100% Offline-First application. We believe that what you earn, spend, and save is your personal business.

We do not collect, transmit, analyze, share, or sell any personal information or financial logs. Everything you record stays strictly on your physical Android device.

2. Zero Data Collection

TrackSpend collects zero personal data:

  • No Account Required: No signup, login, email address, or phone number needed.
  • No Bank Account Linking: We never request or connect to your bank accounts, credit cards, or financial institutions.
  • No Cloud Servers: We operate no external databases or cloud servers for user data.
  • No Tracking & Analytics SDKs: The app contains zero analytics libraries, zero tracking pixels, and zero telemetry tools.
  • No Advertising SDKs: TrackSpend is completely ad-free and contains no third-party ad networks.

3. Local Device Storage & Encryption

All recorded expenses, income entries, categories, monthly budgets, and preferences are stored exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage using Android's secure application storage boundaries.

If you choose to use the Create Backup File (JSON) or Export CSV features, the export is created locally in your device cache and handed directly to your Android system sharing menu. You control where that file is shared.

4. Device Permissions & Biometrics

TrackSpend requests only the following hardware permissions:

  • Biometric Sensors (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Used solely to lock and unlock the app using your device's fingerprint or face sensor. Authentication is performed directly by the Android operating system. TrackSpend never accesses, processes, or stores your biometric data.

5. Data Deletion & Control

You have full and immediate control over your data:

  • Instant Clear: You can wipe all transactions at any time in Settings → DANGER ZONE → Clear All Transactions.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local application data from your device storage.

6. Children's Privacy

TrackSpend does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The application is rated Everyone on Google Play.
